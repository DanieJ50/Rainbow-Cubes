✨ THE LEGEND

THE STARBERRY
PRISM

CHAPTER 01

The Realm Once Glowed as One

At the center of Berry Belle stood the Starberry Prism, a magical fruit-shaped crystal that balanced passion, wisdom, dreams, play, joy, and imagination.

CHAPTER 02

Then Came the Great Pop

One spectacular burst sent six color sparks flying into distant corners of the realm. The world did not become dark — it became wonderfully, chaotically colorful.

CHAPTER 03

The Bunch Found Their Sparks

Each spark chose a berry guardian whose nature matched its power. Together, Ruby, Bluebelle, Plumbleberry, Giggles, Sunny, and Tangerberry keep their regions bright while searching for ways to reconnect the realm.

THE ADVENTURE

Every Puzzle Restores a Little Joylight

The game's colorful matches represent Joylight returning to Berry Belle. Combos create bigger bursts, unlock paths, and help the Bunch bring all six sparks back into harmony.
