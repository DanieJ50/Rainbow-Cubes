03 / THE BERRYLORE

Welcome to Berryvale.

The puzzle board is not just a board. In the lore, every match repairs a tiny piece of a world whose colors have been knocked out of harmony.

I

THE PRISM ORCHARD

Color grew before the first puzzle existed.

At the center of Berryvale grows the Prism Orchard, a living patchwork of berry vines that turns feelings, ideas, and friendship into color. Every district carries a different color energy.

II

THE SOUR STATIC

Then the patterns broke.

A strange force called the Sour Static swept through Berryvale, splitting the orchard's color current into scattered tiles. Paths vanished, gates locked, and once-connected districts became isolated puzzle pockets.

III

THE BERRY BUNCH

Six personalities became one team.

Ruby, Bluebelle, Plumbleberry, Gooseberry, Sunny, and Tangerberry discover that matching the same color creates a Berry Spark — enough shared energy to reconnect broken pieces of the map.

IV

THE PLAYER'S ROLE

You are the Pattern Keeper.

Your moves guide the Bunch. Combos become Chain Blooms, score becomes Harmony, boosters become Berry Bursts, and earned stars reopen the routes leading deeper into Berryvale.
